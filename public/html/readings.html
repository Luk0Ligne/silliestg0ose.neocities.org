Interesting Literature Worth a Read!

I made this page so that I could compile readings that I found interesting, some may be more random than others! Keep in mind this page may be a little barren for a while as reading takes time! Plus I have to parse through what I actually agree with, enjoyed, etc. I hope you fall into some of the rabbitholes I fell through! I am including links to each piece of work I include and some of them I might've even found by looking through other neocities websites, so thank you people for keeping me cultured! :D

Articles:

We Need a Greener Way to Die
- I just thought this was quite informative on the ways we actually "dispose" of our human companions, really puts into perspective how polluting our methods truly are.

PLANTS AS PROTEST: GUERRILLA GARDENING AND ITS ROLE IN URBAN ENVIRONMENTALISM
- An interesting thesis! Explains the history between plants and humans, the positive impacts of plants on humans, the big history of the movement and its positive impacts on urban dwellers. Very informative and helped me get a clear understanding of the movement.

Books:

Bunny 4★
- I found this psychological novel quite enjoyable and I liked that you could interpret the story in two ways depending on your viewpoint. A little dark at times when it comes to animals but nothing I found that was over the top or too graphic.

Butcher & Blackbird Trilogy 4★
- A really good series if your into dark romance like I am! An aspect I really appreciated is that the crimes performed by the characters are only homocidal ones and not sexual ones (looking at you popular dark romance novels T-T).
